# Build seed artwork

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Original characters; supplied app screenshots informed the layout, not copied artwork.

- `public/build-hero-v1.png`: realistic paired-character hero.
- `public/build-toon-covers-v1.png`: three toon-shaded cover portraits.
- `public/build-real-covers-v1.png`: three realistic cover portraits.

Cover atlases are displayed as separate CSS background crops without changing the source assets. The interface adds all titles separately.

## buildHero

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: vertical mobile Build page hero illustration, portrait 3:4 canvas. Original clean anime-inspired character art rendered with highly realistic cinematic materials, soft lifelike skin and cloth detail, tasteful restrained facial proportions. Two adult explorers, a woman with short dark hair and a man with sandy hair, wearing simple contemporary pale sand and charcoal travel clothes. Waist-up pair portrait against a distant curved planetary horizon and sparse desert landscape. Muted dusty atmospheric blue, faded teal, warm pale gold and sand, charcoal shadows. Soft sunrise illumination, low saturation, gentle contrast, luminous but never neon. Elegant calm romance-adventure cover composition, faces in upper middle; leave lower quarter less busy for an HTML title overlay. Not a screenshot or UI. No text, letters, logos, watermark, borders. Original characters, no existing franchise characters.

## buildToon

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: one three-cover horizontal artwork atlas, landscape 3:2 canvas, split into EXACTLY THREE equally wide portrait compositions side by side with boundaries at one-third and two-thirds. No gutters, no drawn borders. Each cover must stay wholly within its own third, centered independent head-and-shoulders portrait. Left: adult woman with short dark hair, calm expression, dusty blue space horizon. Middle: adult man with sandy hair, amber desert cliffs and cream sky. Right: adult woman with dark curly hair, faded teal clouds and sand terrain. Original clean anime-style adult character designs, minimalist toon shading with thin delicate ink contours, broad flat color regions, sparse detail, elegant readable silhouettes. Palette is faded atmospheric blue/teal with warm cream, gold and dusty ochre, muted low saturation and soft contrast, not purple or neon. Portrait poster composition, upper two-thirds faces, lower third simple clothing/background for future titles. No text, logos, watermark, UI or typography. Entire three-part atlas must be complete edge to edge.

## buildReal

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: three-cover horizontal artwork atlas, landscape 3:2 canvas, exactly THREE equal-width independent portrait covers edge to edge, no gutters or borders. Every cover is centered within its own third with no subject crossing divisions. Clean anime-inspired original adult characters translated into high realism: soft lifelike skin, natural hair, detailed simple contemporary clothing, cinematic yet restrained. Left: adult woman with short dark hair in pale sand jacket against orbital blue atmosphere. Middle: adult man with sandy hair against a warm golden desert horizon. Right: adult woman with dark curly hair against subdued teal clouds. Consistent faded muted pale gold, sand, dusty atmospheric blue, charcoal palette matching a realistic Earth sunrise and illustrated desert planet. Soft sunrise light, low saturation, gentle contrast, no glossy neon. Tight chest-up portrait framing, faces clearly visible in upper middle, uncluttered lower region for HTML titles. No text, logos, watermark, UI, no existing fictional characters.
