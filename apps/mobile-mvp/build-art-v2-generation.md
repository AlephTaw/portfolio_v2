# Build art correction — v2

Built-in image-generation tool. Supersedes live v1 artwork; originals retained unused.

- `public/build-hero-toon-v2.png`: simplified toon desert hero and characters.
- `public/build-toon-covers-v2.png`: simple cel-shaded characters.
- `public/build-space-covers-v2.png`: realistic Earth/space scenery with no characters or desert planet.

## heroToon

Use case: style-transfer. Edit target: supplied Build hero. Preserve the two adult explorers, their poses, composition, desert planet landscape, curved planet and sunrise. Convert EVERYTHING to a very simple clean toon-shader illustration: large flat color areas, only two or three tones per material, thin restrained outlines, minimal hair strands, simple anime faces, no skin pores, no photographic cloth, no painterly rendering, no intricate hatching. Muted dusty blue sky, cream clouds, sand and faded golden ochre, charcoal. Desert is exclusively illustrated, never realistic. Characters are simplistic cel-shaded, never realistic. Keep portrait canvas. No text, logos or watermark.

## coversToon

Use case: illustration-story. Asset: horizontal 3:2 artwork atlas containing exactly THREE equal-width independent portrait panels side by side, no gutters or borders. Left an adult woman with short dark hair against blue orbital atmosphere. Middle an adult man with sandy hair against a desert landscape. Right an adult woman with dark curly hair against teal sky and pale desert. All characters and every background in extremely simple clean toon-shader style: broad flat color regions, only 2-3 shade tones, minimal thin contour lines, simple expressive anime faces, hair rendered as large shapes with few strands. No photorealism, pores, detailed fabric, intricate hatching or painterly texture. Muted atmospheric dusty blue, faded teal, pale sand and golden ochre; soft low-saturation harmony. Head-and-shoulders centered in each third with no crossing between panels. No text, symbols, typography, logos or watermark.

## coversSpace

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset: horizontal 3:2 artwork atlas with exactly THREE equally wide portrait compositions side by side, no borders or gutters. Hyperrealistic orbital Earth and space scenery ONLY, absolutely no people or characters, no faces, no creatures, no spacecraft, no desert planet. Left: curved blue Earth limb, ocean and fine cloud systems against pure black space. Middle: golden sunrise cresting Earth's blue atmosphere with realistic cloud formations, soft restrained lens flare. Right: tranquil blue Earth ocean, swirling white cloud formations and distant moon in black space. Muted faded atmospheric blue with pale cream gold highlights, low saturation and soft contrast matching our Earth landing image. Each scene self-contained in its own equal vertical third. No text, logos, watermark, or UI.
