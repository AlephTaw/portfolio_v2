Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: high-resolution landing-page background for a mobile-first app. Create a wholly original hyperrealistic orbital photograph of Earth's curved atmospheric limb at sunrise against pure black deep space. Landscape approximately 4:3, highest available native resolution. A brilliant but tastefully exposed sunrise peeks over the limb, producing realistic optical starburst rays, a fine horizontal lens flare, and faint subdued blue/green/violet lens ghosts toward the lower-left. Dimly illuminated Earth beneath the rim: intricately textured, physically plausible cloud systems, ocean and terrain in deep midnight blue and warm reflected gold, no invented planet features. Preserve deep black negative space above and below the diagonal planet for UI readability. Natural cinematic exposure, refined color harmony, extremely crisp atmospheric scattering and realistic fine cloud detail, not a painting or illustration. Create new cloud arrangements, geography, curvature/framing details and optical flare details, not a pixel-for-pixel recreation of any existing photograph. No text, branding, spacecraft, stars, borders, UI, watermarks or recognizable copyrighted characters. Output one polished original high-resolution image.
