# Life RPG: 30-Day Campaign Map

**Campaign window:** 2026-08-31 through 2026-09-30  
**Category tags:** `health` `wealth` `connection` `sentience` `competence` `experience` `planning`

**Timestamp convention:** `Day 0` = 2026-08-31; `Day 3` = 2026-09-03; `Day 7 / Week 1` = 2026-09-07; `Day 14 / Week 2` = 2026-09-14; `Day 21 / Week 3` = 2026-09-21; `Day 30 / Month 1` = 2026-09-30. Milestones are due by the end of the stated date in Eastern Time.

Levels are priority gates. Quests run in parallel, but higher levels do not unlock until essential lower-level conditions are satisfied.

## Level Map

1. **Level 0 — Spawn Point:** establish an accurate starting state.
2. **Level 1 — Survival Base:** protect food, housing, health, sleep, and immediate obligations.
3. **Level 2 — Financial Stabilization:** create control over bills, spending, and short-term cash flow.
4. **Level 3 — Income Recovery:** secure or materially advance toward reliable income.
5. **Level 4 — Core Competence:** build skills that directly increase earning power.
6. **Level 5 — Sustainable Function:** make health and planning routines repeatable.
7. **Level 6 — Connection Expansion:** deliberately rebuild relationships and community.
8. **Level 7 — Meaning and Mastery:** complete a meaningful challenge and clarify direction.

The one-month objective is to reach **Level 5**, with Levels 6–7 active as controlled secondary quests.

## Daily and weekly game rules

- Select no more than **three priority actions per day**. `planning`
- Protect Levels 1–3 before optional self-development. `planning` `wealth` `health`
- Keep competence work tied to income until Level 3 is secure. `competence` `wealth`
- Expand connection gradually once the survival base is stable. `connection`
- Review alignment, effectiveness, consistency, and blockers every week. `sentience` `planning`
- Advance a level when its unlock condition is met; do not require perfection in every quest.
